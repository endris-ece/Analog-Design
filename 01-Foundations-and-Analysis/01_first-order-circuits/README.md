# First-Order Circuits

## Overview
This project explores the transient responses of first-order RC and RL circuits using LTspice, focusing on charging, discharging, and time constants.

## Experiments

### 1. Series RC Circuit
- Charging and discharging responses.
- Capacitor voltage and resistor current.
- Time constant: \(\tau=RC=1\,s\).

### 2. Series RL Circuit
- Charging and discharging responses.
- Inductor current and voltage.
- Time constant: \(\tau=L/R=0.2\,s\).

## Conclusion
Both circuits exhibit exponential charging and discharging responses, but differ in their energy-storage elements and state variables. The RC circuit stores energy in the capacitor's electric field, with capacitor voltage changing gradually. The RL circuit stores energy in the inductor's magnetic field, with inductor current changing gradually. Their time constants determine how quickly they respond to changes in the input.

## Tools
- LTspice

## Objectives
- Understand first-order circuit behavior.
- Verify theoretical time constants through simulation.
- Investigate how component values affect transient responses.
